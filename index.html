Good to Know

Questions & Policies

How do I book?

Use the form below or message us on WhatsApp. We will confirm your slot by reply. Your booking is confirmed only after we respond.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

For longer treatments such as semi-permanent makeup, a deposit may be required to hold your slot. We will tell you the amount when we confirm.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please let us know at least 24 hours before your appointment if you need to reschedule. Late cancellations may affect your deposit.

Is a patch test needed?

Yes, for treatments like lash extensions and tinting we recommend a patch test, especially if you have sensitive skin or allergies. Tell us about any allergies when you book.

How should I care for my results?

We give aftercare instructions at the end of every service so your results last longer and heal well.
